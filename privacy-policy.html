PRIVACY · UPDATED 20 OCTOBER 2026

Your work.
On your device.

Further Compare processes the text and files you compare locally. You do not need an account.

Online text comparison

Text you paste or open is processed in your browser. The comparison code does not upload that content to a server. Keep a copy of important text: the online workspace is not a storage service.

Desktop files and folders

The desktop app reads the files and folders you select and performs comparison and approved file operations on your computer. Saving a merged file, copying files, or synchronizing a folder changes local files. Files in a cloud-synced location may also be synchronized by that separate service.

Website connections

Your browser requests this website’s HTML, scripts, styles, and download availability information from the host. Like other website hosts, the hosting provider may retain access logs containing information such as IP addresses, request times, and browser details. File contents are not part of these requests.

Downloading a desktop build connects you to the download host. The product pages do not include third-party fonts, analytics, advertising scripts, or account sign-in.

Your control

You choose the files to open, the changes to apply, and where to save exported results. Closing the workspace releases its active comparison state. Downloads and files you save remain on your device until you remove them.

Scope

This policy describes the Further Compare application and website code distributed with this release. It does not govern your operating system, browser extensions, cloud storage provider, or the policies of a separate site hosting a copy of this tool.
