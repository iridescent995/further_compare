Further Compare brings text and folder comparison into one simple workspace.

Made for the details

A changed setting. A revised document. Two folders that have drifted apart. Further Compare helps you understand the differences and decide what to carry forward.

The online edition offers focused, local text comparison with a limit of 2 MB and 30,000 lines per side. The desktop edition adds text merging and folder comparison, merge, and synchronization.

Local by design

The online comparator processes text in your browser. The desktop app works with files on your computer. Neither comparison workflow needs an account or sends file contents to a comparison server. Read the privacy policy for details.

A preview, built to be useful

The desktop app is distributed as unsigned portable preview builds for macOS and Windows. Review proposed changes before applying file operations, and keep a separate backup of important work.
